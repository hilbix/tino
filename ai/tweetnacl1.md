is tweetnacl quantum safe

No, **TweetNaCl** is **not quantum safe** (post-quantum secure). It is a classical cryptographic library based on algorithms like Curve25519 and Ed25519, which are vulnerable to attacks by quantum computers.

*   Official documentation explicitly states that **TweetNaCl** and **NaCl** are **not post-quantum secure**.
*   It is designed for use in **hybrid transition phases** alongside post-quantum algorithms (such as ML-KEM and ML-DSA) or where quantum resistance is not yet required.
*   While highly secure against classical computing attacks, **quantum computing** poses a direct threat to the elliptic curve cryptography primitives used by **TweetNaCl**.
